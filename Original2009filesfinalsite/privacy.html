Privacy Statement


Information Collected

We collect contact information you give to us through enquiries you have made and every time you e-mail us or post on our guestbook.


Your Privacy

The Penicuik Historical Society is committed to safeguarding the privacy of our users. We will only use the information we collect about you lawfully (in accordance with the Data Protection Act 1998) for example when replying to you through our e-mail service. If you have any requests concerning your personal information or any queries with regard to this practice please contact us by e-mail at info@penicuikpapermaking.org or in writing to:

Penicuik Historical Society
Penicuik Town Hall
33 High Street
Penicuik
EH26 8HS
Midlothian
Scotland

Use of Personal Information

We process personal information collected via this website for the purposes of answering questions you have submitted through our e-mail service and to maintain accurate communication with you as a visitor to our website.


Disclosures

We do not pass your information to third parties other than those involved in answering any questions you have sent to us through our e-mail service.

If you have any queries about our Privacy Statement please contact us by e-mail at info@penicuikpapermaking.org
